Learning center

🕯️ Candle basics

A candle records open, high, low and close. A bullish candle closes above its open; a bearish candle closes below it.

📈 EMA9 & EMA21

EMA9 responds faster than EMA21. Crossovers can help identify momentum but also create false signals in sideways markets.

📉 RSI

RSI estimates momentum. It is not a standalone buy/sell signal; consider trend and candle context.

🛡️ Risk rules

Never risk money you cannot afford to lose. Avoid revenge trading and remember that past results do not predict future results.
